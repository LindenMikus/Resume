\section*{Experience}

\jobheader{Sr. Software Engineer}{Jan 2022 -- Aug 2026}{Shift4 Payments / Worldline}{Victoria, BC}
{\small\color{textgray!80}\textit{Full-Stack engineer leading projects to completion across three platforms (e-commerce and billing, card present gateway, merchant onboarding) in a 24/7 operation (Bambora division).}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Migrated .NET Framework applications to .NET Core incrementally: parallel SDK-style projects linking the original sources, compile-time symbols for runtime differences, Core-only replacements where files could not cross, then app-by-app cutover onto a supported cross-platform runtime in Linux containers, with no freeze on feature work.
  \item Supported end-to-end payment processing across card-present (EMV chip, NFC/contactless, magnetic stripe), card-not-present, and e-commerce flows on a platform serving 500+ ISVs and 140K+ total merchants.
  \item Developed batch payment services on a bank-to-bank platform moving over \$200M a day across ACH, EFT, SEPA and BACS, in both credit and debit directions, validating generated bank files against the NACHA and Canadian CPA 1464 specifications.
  \item Designed a reusable multifactor authentication framework for PCI 4.0 compliance, shipping a shared NuGet package and web UI integrations across three merchant and staff portals, rolled out to 140k merchants, with a delegated permission model letting parent accounts enforce enrollment and issue MFA resets for users beneath them.
  \item Defined specifications and delivered merchant onboarding APIs automating merchant creation and pricing configuration, cutting manual setup by 1 hour per merchant, with contract-first OpenAPI specs and NSwag-generated clients shared across three APIs and two UIs.
  \item Designed and owned a pricing package API from spec to production, moving merchant pricing from per-merchant configuration to reusable channel-partner packages (buy rate, sell rate, spread, revenue share), with an isolated billing schema writable only by the API, versioned stored procedures for zero-downtime releases, and a source-generated Dapper bulk import over table-valued parameters.
  \item Led migration of 13k merchant bank accounts and mandates to a new SEPA/BACS banking provider before a hard bank cutoff: reworked the account/mandate schema from one-to-one to one-to-many, migrated the data, and bulk-created mandates through the mandate API, maintaining settlement continuity and ISO 20022 regulatory compliance.
  \item Integrated a third-party sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) screening service into the batch payment scheduler for 250k transactions monthly: parallel screening (10 concurrent requests per batch), cached JWT auth with refresh on 401, retry with exponential backoff and jitter, vendor-deduplicated idempotency keys, an automatic fail-safe that disables screening on sustained vendor failure while preserving the audit record, and a per-transaction audit trail feeding daily manual review.
  \item Developed and launched a merchant onboarding solution with integrated identity verification, onboarding about 1,000 merchants a month and cutting fraud and risk screening by 30 minutes per merchant, and owned its lifecycle through to production on Kubernetes and ArgoCD.
  \item Implemented structured logging, dashboards, alerting, and tracing across services; identified sensitive data reaching log output and authored the logging and validation-handling standards that closed it.
  \item Extended a Gherkin end-to-end test automation suite, adding new scenarios and features across onboarding, payments, and authentication flows, while improving system-wide test coverage from 20\% to 60\%.
  \item Ran release demos for pricing packages and MFA, and produced AI-assisted wireframes and technical documents.
  \item Built a Docker Compose repository that stands up the entire platform stack on a developer machine, then taught and onboarded engineers from other teams onto it.
  \item Delivered an end-to-end solution for flexible channel partner commission payouts to multiple school ISV businesses to assist in increasing cash flow from monthly to as low as daily and provide API/UI reporting with additional statement details for reconciliation.
\end{itemize}
