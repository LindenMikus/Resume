  \item Supported end-to-end payment processing across card-present (EMV chip, NFC/contactless, magnetic stripe), card-not-present, and e-commerce flows on a platform serving 500+ ISVs and 140K+ total merchants.

  \item Delivered an end-to-end solution for flexible channel partner commission payouts to multiple school ISV businesses to assist in increasing cash flow from monthly to as low as daily and provide API/UI reporting with additional statement details for reconciliation.
